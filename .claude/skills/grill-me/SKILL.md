# Grill Me Workflow

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. 

### Rules of Engagement:
1. **One Question at a Time:** Do not overwhelm me with a bulleted list of 5 things. Ask one hyper-focused question, wait for my response, and iterate.
2. **Provide Your Recommendation:** For each question you pitch, provide your own optimal recommendation inline so I can choose to agree with you or override it, speeding up the architecture session.
3. **Puncture Gaps:** Actively look for edge cases, missing error boundaries, hidden dependencies, or scaling limitations in what I propose.
